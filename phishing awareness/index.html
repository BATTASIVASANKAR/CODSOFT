Phishing Awareness Program

Understanding phishing is the first step to staying safe online.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a trick where attackers pretend to be trustworthy sources to steal personal or confidential information.

Types of Phishing

  • Email Phishing – generic emails that appear to come from a reputable company.
  • Spear Phishing – targeted attacks aimed at a specific person or organization.
  • Smishing – phishing attempts sent via SMS/text messages.
  • Vishing – voice‑based phishing, often carried out over the phone.

How to Identify Phishing

  • Suspicious emails – check sender address and look for generic greetings.
  • Fake login pages – verify the URL and look for misspellings.
  • Fraudulent websites – hover over links to see the real destination.
  • Suspicious links – never click links you do not trust.
  • Unknown attachments – do not open unexpected files.
  • Urgent messages – beware of pressure to act quickly.

Social Engineering Techniques

Attackers often exploit human psychology. Common techniques include:

  • Pretexting – creating a fabricated scenario to obtain information.
  • Baiting – offering something enticing to lure victims.
  • Tailgating – following an authorized person into a restricted area.
  • Quid pro quo – promising a benefit in exchange for information.

Prevention Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords for each service.
  • Enable Two‑Factor Authentication wherever possible.
  • Verify website URLs before entering credentials.
  • Avoid clicking on unknown or suspicious links.
  • Keep software and operating systems up to date.
